Library Desk

Bookshelves that hold books, scrolls, journals, card boxes and blueprints. Pages take rich text, drawings, pasted images and text boxes — and the paper always stays the focus, with the tools in the toolbar and the rail rather than crowding the page.

The tools

File Workshop

Convert, clean and organize class files without leaving Makullveny. Every conversion runs locally through a bundled FFmpeg — nothing is uploaded, and nothing needs an account.

New · a photo, into flashcards

Snap your index cards. Study them tonight.

Put a photo of your handwritten cards on a Library Desk page and press Extract text. Your own computer reads it — nothing is uploaded — and you fix anything it misread. Highlight a term, press Card, and the deck is waiting in Flashcards.

Import Desk

Drop a syllabus in, pick the term, tick what to keep, and your classes, dates and a starter deck arrive in five short steps.

Typing Trials

Practice your typing and watch the speed climb. Timed or word-count runs, live WPM and accuracy, records kept on this device.

Cloud Backpack New

Optional backup for your library, so the books on one computer come with you to the next. Off until you switch it on, and never needed to write.

Calculator

A scientific calculator that works with no connection at all — trig, logs, factorials, powers, and a keyboard shortcut of your choosing.

Sound

A lofi radio and room ambience, built in.

The radio pops out into its own little window with a real visualizer, so the music stays where you can reach it. Ambience layers underneath. And if movement is the thing that distracts you, a reduced-motion mode turns all of it off.
