Quick answers

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password?" and follow the email.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile, their Study Team, the study call or the wave, and choose Report or Block. See the Community Guidelines.

How do I delete my data or my account?

Signed in, open Settings > Account > Delete. You can delete only your Makullveny data, or your whole TDG account. The files on your device always stay. More in the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel Lantern?

On a computer: Settings > Account > Manage billing. On iPhone: your Apple account's Subscriptions settings. You keep Lantern until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Candle bought on a computer can be refunded within 7 days of buying, for any reason: Settings > Account > Payment history. Purchases made on iPhone are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. The rest is in the Terms, section A10.

Where is my work stored?

On your own device. Use Export in Settings to make a copy any time.

Found a security problem?

Thank you. Email us the details and please don't share them publicly until we have fixed it.
