Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026. Goes with the Makullveny agreement, version 2.2.

The short version

  • Your notes, books, pages and drawings stay on your own device. We cannot read them.
  • Nothing about you reaches us until you sign in. After that, most things are off until you turn them on. Two are on: a few usage counts (you can switch them off) and your place on the public Focus Points and Selah leaderboards (you can show as Anonymous).
  • We never sell your data. There are no ads and no trackers in the app.
  • You can delete your data, or your whole account, from inside the app at any time.
  • Questions? Email hello@makullveny.com. A real person reads it.

Who we are

Makullveny is a study app made by Nate McIlvenny and Luke McIlvenny, doing business as TDG. When this page says "we" or "us", it means us. We decide what happens to your data, and we are the ones to ask about it.

What stays on your device

Makullveny is local-first. These live only on your own computer or phone, in files that belong to you:

  • your books, pages, drawings, notes and quick notes
  • your flashcards, imported files and the documents you read in
  • your settings and the app's own backups
  • the calendar link you paste from your school. It works like a password, so it is locked on your device and never sent to us, never put in an export, and never shown back to you.

None of this leaves your device unless you choose one of the things below that sends it.

What reaches our server, and why

With no account

Nothing about you. The app still asks a few public services for things you need (see "Who helps us" below), and those see only an ordinary web request.

Every time the app talks to a server

Like every website, the server sees your device's internet address (IP address). Our hosts keep it in short-term logs that help keep the service safe and working. We never use it to track you.

Your TDG account (if you make one)

One TDG account signs you in to every TDG app. It holds your email address, your username, a display name and a picture if you choose them, a short bio if you write one, your timezone if you allow it, and what you have bought. It never holds your writing.

Things you turn on

  • Cloud Backpack. The books and items you pick are copied to our server so you can get them back on another device. Only what you pick goes in.
  • Classes and assignments that follow you. Your class names, rooms, times, links and assignment cards can be kept with your account so your other devices show them too.
  • Your Selah island. Your island's save can be kept with your account.
  • Pictures in books (Lantern and up). Pictures in a book you keep in your Backpack are stored with our picture-storage provider.
  • Friends, Study Teams, study calls and waves. To make these work, we keep who your friends are, the teams you are on, the team name and motto, the study call messages you send (up to 90 characters), and the waves you send. See "What other people can see".
  • The typing leaderboard. If you join it, your best score and username are shown on a board.
  • Feedback and reports. When you send us feedback or report something, we keep what you wrote so we can answer and act on it.

Usage counts

While you are signed in, the app sends a few numbers each day: how often it was opened, for how long, which parts were used, how many Lock Ins you started and finished, the app version and whether your device runs Windows, Mac, Linux or iPhone. These never include your notes, class names, assignment names, file names, search words, or anything you type. You can switch them off in Settings, see exactly what the next send holds, and erase what was already sent. On iPhone, the app asks you first.

If you also turn on "Help improve Makullveny" (off unless you turn it on), the counts include finer details, like which buttons you used and how many errors the app caught.

Payments

On a computer, Stripe takes the payment. On iPhone, Apple takes it through In-App Purchase, and a service called RevenueCat tells us what your purchase unlocks. Your card details go to Stripe or Apple, never to us.

Emails

A signed-in account gets a welcome email, and a receipt when you buy something. These account emails always come. News emails and the Lantern renewal reminder only come if you turn them on, and each of those has a link to stop it. We never give your email address to advertisers.

What other people can see

  • Your friends see your profile (name, username, picture and bio), your status (like "In class" or "Locked in"), and anything you choose to share with them, like your classes or your Selah island. Friends are by request only. Nobody is your friend unless you both say yes.
  • Everyone using Makullveny can see the Focus Points and Selah leaderboards. While you are signed in, your username, your theme, your Focus Points and your Selah level show there. Would you rather not? Turn on Show me as Anonymous in Community. Then anyone who is not your friend sees "Anonymous" instead, and your friends still see you.
  • Your Study Team sees the team's name, motto and members, and the team's study calls. A team its owner lists can appear on a public team or school page on makullveny.com. A member is named there only if they already show by username on the public leaderboard.
  • People you may know are friends of your friends, Study Team mates and people at your school. You only show up there if your TDG profile lets people find you, and they see your name, username and picture, nothing more.
  • A link you make (a profile link or a shared page) can be opened by anyone who has it. You can turn it off at any time.
  • No chat with strangers. There is no chat with strangers and no random or anonymous chat.

Keeping people safe

You can report or block anyone from their profile, a suggestion, a Study Team, a study call or a wave. When you report something, we keep who reported, what was reported, the reason, and a copy of what it said at the time, so a real person at TDG can look at it. The person you report is never told who reported them. Names, mottos, bios and study call messages are checked by a word filter before they are saved.

Who helps us

A few companies run parts of Makullveny for us. Each one gets only what its job needs, and none of them may use it for anything else.

  • Supabase runs our database and sign-in.
  • Backblaze stores pictures in books.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Stripe takes payments on computers.
  • Apple takes payments on iPhone, and RevenueCat keeps track of what those purchases unlock.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app downloads, and answers the app's update checks.
  • Google and GitHub sign-in, only if you choose one of them to sign in on a computer. They tell us your email address and name.

Some public services answer questions only when the app asks: the verse of the day, a citation lookup you start (Crossref and Open Library), and the school search when you pick your school (public U.S. Department of Education data). They get an ordinary web request and nothing about you.

We do not sell or rent your data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI.

We may share data if the law truly requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and the things you turned on: until you delete them or your account.
  • Usage counts: at most 400 days. The finer counts: at most 90 days.
  • Reports: while we look into them, and up to 12 months after they are closed, so we can spot a pattern. Then they are deleted.
  • Pictures in books: removed from storage within a few days of being deleted.
  • A note that data was deleted (which option you chose and when, with no name or email): 1 year, so we can answer questions about it.
  • Payment records: Stripe and Apple keep their own records, as the law requires them to.
  • Backups: our database host may keep short-term backups. A deleted thing can stay in one of those for up to 30 days before it is gone for good.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open Settings > Account and choose one of two options:

  • Delete my Makullveny data only. Removes everything Makullveny keeps for you on our server: your Cloud Backpack, synced classes and assignments, Selah save, pictures, Study Teams you own, study calls, waves, reports you sent and usage counts. It also turns off any link you made. Your TDG account and friends list stay, so other TDG apps keep working, and a record of what you bought stays so it is still yours.
  • Delete my account from ALL TDG apps. Deletes your TDG account itself, in every TDG app. This cannot be undone. If a paid plan is still renewing, cancel it first. The app shows you where.

Either way, the files on your own device stay. They are yours. If you can't sign in, email us and we will help.

If you subscribed on iPhone, deleting does not stop Apple's billing on its own. Cancel the subscription in your Apple account settings too.

Students under 18

You must be at least 13 to use Makullveny. If you are under 18, use it with your parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Some app stores, including Apple's, can tell an app a person's age range, or ask a parent to approve a change. Where that is offered, we use the answer only to decide which features fit, like whether friends and Study Teams are on by default.

Your choices and rights

  • See and copy your work any time with Export, in Settings.
  • Fix your name, username, picture and bio in your profile.
  • Turn off usage counts, emails, sharing with friends and the typing leaderboard in Settings, and show as Anonymous on the other leaderboards in Community.
  • Delete your data or your account, as above.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or anything else this page covers. We will answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), the law may give you more rights. We honour them. Email us to use them.

Keeping it safe

Everything between the app and our server is encrypted. Every person's data on our server is fenced off so only they, and the people they chose, can read it. TDG's admins (today, just the two of us) can see account details, reports and usage totals when they need to help you or keep people safe. No system is perfect. If something ever goes wrong with your data, we will tell you.

Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top changes. If a change takes away something this page promised, the app tells you before it applies to you.

Contact
