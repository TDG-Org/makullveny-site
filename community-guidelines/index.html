Community Guidelines

Last updated 15 October 2026. Part of the Makullveny agreement (section A20).

Makullveny is a place to study, with friends who help you show up. These guidelines cover everything other people can read: your profile and bio, Study Team names and mottos, study calls and waves.

Be kind

  • Cheer people on. Everyone is working on something hard.
  • Use names and words you would be fine with a teacher or parent reading.
  • Respect "no". If someone doesn't want to be friends or join your team, let it go.

Never

  • Bully, threaten, mock or pile on anyone.
  • Post hate about who someone is: race, religion, gender, disability, or anything like that.
  • Post anything sexual, or ask anyone for pictures.
  • Share someone else's private information, like their address, phone number or passwords.
  • Pretend to be someone else.
  • Spam, sell things, or send people to other websites they didn't ask for.
  • Encourage anyone to hurt themselves or others.
  • Help anyone cheat, or post answers to tests.

How Makullveny is built to keep you safe

  • Friends are by request. Nobody is your friend unless you both say yes.
  • Study Teams are by invite or code.
  • There is no chat with strangers, and no random or anonymous chat.
  • Names, mottos, bios and study calls are checked for words that are not allowed. If yours is stopped, try different words.

If something is wrong

  • Report it. Open the person's profile, the team, the study call or the wave, and choose Report. It disappears from your screen right away. A real person at TDG reads every report, and we aim to act within 24 hours. The person is never told who reported them.
  • Block them. Choose Block in the same places. They leave your friends, your study calls, your suggestions and any Study Team you own, and they can't wave to you.
  • Tell someone. If you feel unsafe, tell a parent, a teacher or another adult you trust. If someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number.

What we do

When something breaks these guidelines, we may remove it, turn off a feature for that account, or suspend or close the account. We look at what happened, and we try to be fair.
