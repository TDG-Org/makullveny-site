Makullveny works without one. An account is what lets you add friends, see when they are in class or free, share a profile link, and carry your Backpack to another computer.

  • Friends by @username — see their classes, and who is free right now
  • Your profile link — you choose what it shows
  • Same account in the app — sign in there with this email or username

Your notes, books and assignments still stay on your computer. An account never uploads them.
