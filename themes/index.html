Themes

Seventeen rooms,
in three bundles.

Every theme in Makullveny re-skins the entire app — background art, paper, ink, typography and chrome move together. They come in three bundles: four Included with the free app, six in the Candle bundle from the in-app Theme Market, and seven in the Illustrated Art collection, which is on the way. Walk through the first ten below, room by room.

The collection

Bundle two: Illustrated Art.

Seven rooms drawn rather than tinted. Each one is a painted environment with its own light, its own paper and its own furniture — a crimson studio, a marble reading room, a deep-sea gallery, a gilded card table, a city loft at dusk, a candy-soft daylight room, and a greenhouse under northern lights.

Coming with the next tiers

Every room below is already drawn, built and in the app — what is not ready yet is the tier that carries it. The Illustrated Art collection will be included in the tiers above Candle, arriving as the features behind them land. Nothing here is on sale today, and nothing here is a mock-up: this is the collection waiting for its door to open.
